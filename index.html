About us

A product company, built around sport.

We believe the way people follow, understand and talk about sport is changing, and that well-made software can make every match more meaningful.

HECRON LTD is the company behind SensibleStats and FanAmigo. We combine sports data, applied artificial intelligence and careful product design to build tools that fans enjoy using and that businesses can rely on.

We are a private limited company registered in England and Wales, headquartered in London.

Applied AI

Conversational agents and assistants that turn complex sports information into clear, natural answers.

Sports data

Reliable match, team and player data, structured and delivered for real-time experiences.

Product design

Business platforms and consumer apps built with care for usability, performance and privacy.
